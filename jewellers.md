# **11\. JEWELLERY EDUCATION**

**SEO title:** Gold Purity, Karats & Jewellery Buying Guide \| SSA Jewellers

**Meta description:** Learn about gold purity, 18K, 22K and 24K gold, hallmarking, diamond characteristics, and how to make informed jewellery choices with SSA Jewellers.

## **Hero**

# **The more you understand, the more confident you can be.**

Jewellery is a considered purchase.

It may carry emotional significance, financial importance, or both. Understanding the details helps you appreciate what you are choosing and why it may be right for you.

At SSA Jewellers, we believe knowledge should be part of the jewellery-buying experience.

This guide introduces some of the questions worth asking when choosing gold and diamond jewellery.

## **Understanding Gold Purity**

Gold purity indicates the proportion of gold in a metal alloy.

Pure gold is relatively soft, so jewellery is often made by combining gold with other metals to achieve the properties required for its intended use.

### **What Is 24-Karat Gold?**

24K gold represents gold of very high purity. Its suitability depends on the intended product and design.

### **What Is 22-Karat Gold?**

22K gold contains a higher proportion of gold than 18K gold and is commonly used in traditional gold jewellery.

### **What Is 18-Karat Gold?**

18K gold consists of 75% gold, with the remaining 25% made up of other metals. It can be suitable for a variety of jewellery designs, depending on the construction and intended use.

**Important:** The exact purity of a piece should be verified through its applicable hallmarking and product documentation.

## **What Is Gold Hallmarking?**

Hallmarking is a process used to indicate the purity of precious-metal articles under an applicable hallmarking system.

When purchasing gold jewellery, customers should understand the markings and documentation applicable to the piece.

Ask the jeweller to explain the purity, relevant hallmark, and associated documentation before making your decision.

## **How Should You Choose Gold Jewellery?**

The right choice depends on several factors.

**Purpose:** Is the jewellery intended for everyday wear, a celebration, or long-term preservation?

**Design:** Do you prefer a traditional style, a contemporary design, or something that brings both together?

**Budget:** What amount are you comfortable spending?

**Purity:** Which gold purity is appropriate for the type of jewellery you are considering?

**Documentation:** What hallmarking and purchase documentation accompany the piece?

**Maintenance:** What care will help preserve the jewellery’s appearance over time?

A thoughtful purchase begins with understanding these considerations.

## **Understanding Diamonds**

The appearance and characteristics of a diamond are commonly assessed through four factors known as the 4Cs:

* **Cut:** The quality of the diamond’s cut, which influences how it interacts with light.  
* **Colour:** The degree to which a diamond is colourless or displays colour, according to the grading system used.  
* **Clarity:** The presence and characteristics of internal inclusions and external blemishes.  
* **Carat:** A unit of measurement for a diamond’s weight.

These factors work together. No single characteristic tells the entire story of a diamond.

Ask about the grading documentation available for the diamond jewellery you are considering.

## **Questions Worth Asking Before You Buy**

* What is the gold purity of this piece?  
* What hallmarking or certification applies?  
* How are the gold weight and making charges calculated?  
* Are any stones included, and how are they described?  
* What documentation will I receive?  
* How should I care for this jewellery?  
* What after-sales services are available?

These are sensible questions to ask when making a considered jewellery purchase.

At SSA Jewellers, we believe customers should feel comfortable asking them.

**Because an informed decision is a more confident decision.**
